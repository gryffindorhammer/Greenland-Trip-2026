✍️ The Greenland Plan

A short trip should have one anchor experience and enough open space to let the place, weather, and energy set the rest.

This version stays in Nuuk rather than trying to cram Ilulissat into a five-night window. That trade buys fewer connections, a real buffer for Greenlandic weather, and time to notice the city rather than only transit through it.

The anchor is Wednesday afternoon: a six-hour run into the Nuuk Icefjord, with icebergs and the Narsap Sermia glacier as the payoff. The rest is intentionally lighter—harbor walks, museum time, food, a possible hike, and an open Saturday.

That is the right shape for this trip: a strong headline, modest logistics, and no heroics. See the live itinerary.
