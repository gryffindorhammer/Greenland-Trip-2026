Working draft for a long-weekend Greenland sampler using the United nonstop between Newark and Nuuk.

## Trip Shape

- Dates: Tuesday, August 4 to Sunday, August 9, 2026
- Main destination: Nuuk, Greenland
- Style: Nuuk-focused trip with fjord, city, food, hiking/viewpoints, and weather buffer
- Recommendation: do not add Ilulissat on this short schedule

## Day-by-Day Plan

### Tuesday, August 4 - Fly to Nuuk

- Fly Newark to Nuuk.
- Arrive in the evening.
- Keep arrival logistics simple: check in, easy dinner, short waterfront/harbor walk if timing and weather allow.

### Wednesday, August 5 - Nuuk City

- National Museum of Greenland.
- Old harbor and colonial harbor area.
- Central Nuuk walk, cafes, shops, and waterfront viewpoints.
- Keep the day flexible to absorb jet lag and weather.

### Thursday, August 6 - Nuuk Fjord Boat Tour

- Main excursion day: Nuuk fjord boat tour.
- Prioritize a reputable boat operator and book early once flights are confirmed.
- Keep evening open for dinner and recovery.

Google Maps:
- Nuuk Fjord: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nuuk%20Fjord%2C%20Greenland

### Friday, August 7 - Hiking, Viewpoints, or Optional Boat Excursion

- Hike/viewpoint day if weather is good.
- Possible focus: Lille Malene / Quassussuaq area, depending on conditions and trail comfort.
- Alternative: another boat excursion if Thursday is weathered out or if a specific tour looks better.

Google Maps:
- Quassussuaq / Lille Malene: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Quassussuaq%20Lille%20Malene%2C%20Nuuk

### Saturday, August 8 - Weather Buffer / Second Excursion

- Keep this as the built-in buffer day.
- Use it for the fjord tour if Thursday was canceled.
- If weather cooperates and Thursday went well, consider another boat trip, a settlement-focused excursion, more city time, or a slow food/waterfront day.

### Sunday, August 9 - Fly Home

- Morning flight from Nuuk to Newark.
- Leave the day after return free if possible, because Greenland travel can be affected by weather delays.

## Practical Notes

- This schedule is four full days in Nuuk, not five: Tuesday is arrival evening and Sunday is departure morning.
- This is a reasonable long-weekend Greenland sampler, especially if using United travel credit.
- Book refundable or flexible lodging where possible.
- Do not make tight downstream plans immediately after returning.
- Greenland is expensive. Hotels, meals, and boat tours add up quickly.
- Weather can disrupt flights and boat excursions.

## Why Not Ilulissat on This Version

Ilulissat and Disko Bay are the stronger classic first-trip Greenland experience for icebergs, but adding Ilulissat to August 4-9 would make the trip too fragile. Internal Greenland flights are expensive, limited, and weather-sensitive. With only four full days, adding Ilulissat turns the trip into airport roulette.

If Ilulissat is the priority, make the trip 7-9 days or route through Iceland with Ilulissat as the main destination.
