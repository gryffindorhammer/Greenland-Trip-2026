Planning Notes

Context for the current Nuuk-only plan, not a list of active bookings.

Current itinerary: Aug 4–9 in Nuuk, based at HHE Express Hotel, with the Nuuk Icefjord Boat Tour booked for Wed Aug 5 at 3:30 PM. See the live itinerary.

Why this version works

  • Early August brings long daylight, active boat tours, accessible hiking, and relatively mild weather for Greenland.
  • Four full days is enough for Nuuk city time, the icefjord, viewpoints, food, and weather flexibility.
  • Weather can disrupt flights and boats, so the itinerary avoids tight connections and leaves optional time open.

Why not Ilulissat this time

Ilulissat and Disko Bay are the stronger classic first-trip choice for large icebergs, but adding them to this short trip would introduce limited, expensive, weather-sensitive internal connections. Nuuk-only is the sensible, lower-stress version.

Future longer-trip option

For a 7–9 day Greenland trip, pair 4–5 nights in Ilulissat (icefjord, Eqi Glacier, Sermermiut) with 2–3 nights in Nuuk and a weather buffer.

Remaining checks

  • Reconfirm final United times, baggage rules, and seats.
  • Keep hotel cancellation details accessible.
  • Check the boat operator’s weather/cancellation guidance shortly before departure.
