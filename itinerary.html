Nuuk Itinerary

Tuesday, August 4 to Sunday, August 9, 2026

← Trip overview

Tue Aug 4

Fly to Nuuk

Fly Newark (EWR) to Nuuk (GOH), arrive in the evening, and check in at HHE Express Hotel. Keep the evening simple: dinner and a short harbor walk if timing and weather allow.

Wed Aug 5

Nuuk city + icefjord tour

Morning/early afternoon: National Museum, old/colonial harbor, central walk, cafés, and waterfront viewpoints.

Confirmed: Nuuk Icefjord Boat Tour — 3:30 PM
Order #81500 · six-hour icefjord/glacier excursion · meeting point. Allow time for check-in and plan a simple late dinner.

National Museum · Colonial Harbor

Thu Aug 6

Central Nuuk, culture, and an easy harbor walk

Keep the day sheltered and low-commitment after the late icefjord return: Katuaq (with Cafétuaq), Kalaaliaraq / Brættet market, then Anori Art and Qiviut if shopping is appealing. Finish with the Colonial Harbor / Myggedalen walk if the wind is comfortable. Save Radiofjeldet for a calmer window.

Fri Aug 7

Lille Malene / Quassussuaq hike

Make this the active day if visibility, trail conditions, and rain cooperate. Take a bus or taxi to the trail area; the marked 6.4 km circular route is roughly 2½–3 hours. Start in the morning, bring warm windproof layers, water, snacks, and proper shoes, and only continue higher if the weather is stable. Easy dinner afterward.

Quassussuaq / Lille Malene map

Sat Aug 8

Settlement boat day — Kapisillit first choice

Use the best boat-weather window for a full-day Kapisillit trip: it offers a genuinely different experience from Wednesday’s icefjord tour—deep fjord travel and a living settlement. Book only after confirming the exact route, landing time, and weather policy. If a full day is unavailable or conditions are poor, choose a shorter guided Kangeq trip, or do Radiofjeldet → Boardwalk → Colonial Harbor as the no-booking backup.

Sun Aug 9

Fly home

Morning flight from Nuuk to Newark. Leave the next day light if possible: Greenland weather can affect travel.

Practical notes

  • Base: HHE Express Hotel, Nuuk
  • Four full days in Nuuk; Tuesday is arrival evening and Sunday is departure morning.
  • Keep plans flexible and avoid tight downstream commitments after the return flight.
  • Nuuk-only is the right call for this short trip; Ilulissat would make the itinerary too fragile.
