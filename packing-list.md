# Greenland Trip 2026 — Nuuk Packing List

For **Aug 4–9 in Nuuk**: expect cool, changeable weather (often roughly 40s–50s °F / 5–15 °C), wind, occasional rain, long daylight, a fjord boat day, and optional hiking. The key is layers and waterproof outerwear—not heavy expedition gear.

## Wear / Pack

### Outer layers

- [ ] Waterproof, windproof rain shell with hood
- [ ] Insulated jacket (light-to-medium down or synthetic puffer)
- [ ] Midlayer: fleece or warm sweater
- [ ] 2–3 base layers / long-sleeve shirts (merino or synthetic work well)
- [ ] 2–3 T-shirts
- [ ] 1–2 pairs of long pants suitable for walking/hiking (quick-dry preferred)
- [ ] Comfortable outfit for dinners and city time
- [ ] Sleepwear and underwear for six nights
- [ ] 5–7 pairs of warm socks; include 1–2 wool pairs

### Footwear

- [ ] Waterproof hiking shoes or boots with good tread — most useful item for wet trails and uneven ground
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes or sneakers for Nuuk
- [ ] Optional: lightweight camp/hotel shoes or slippers

### Weather accessories

- [ ] Warm beanie
- [ ] Light gloves
- [ ] Buff/neck gaiter or scarf
- [ ] Sunglasses and sunscreen (long daylight and water glare)
- [ ] Reusable water bottle
- [ ] Small daypack (about 15–25 L) with rain cover or dry bag
- [ ] Compact umbrella only for city use; do not rely on it in wind

## Fjord Boat Day

- [ ] Add an extra warm layer; it will feel colder on the water
- [ ] Waterproof shell and waterproof footwear
- [ ] Beanie, gloves, sunglasses, sunscreen
- [ ] Phone/camera plus power bank
- [ ] Small dry bag or zip-top bags for electronics
- [ ] Seasickness medication or bands, if useful
- [ ] Snacks; confirm whether the operator provides food, water, and flotation gear

## Hiking / Viewpoint Day

- [ ] Download offline maps before leaving Wi-Fi
- [ ] Daypack with water, snacks, shell, insulating layer, hat, and gloves
- [ ] Basic blister kit and any personal first-aid items
- [ ] Portable charger
- [ ] Trekking poles, only if you normally use them; no need to buy them solely for this trip

## Travel Essentials

- [ ] Passport, plus a photo/secure copy
- [ ] Flight, hotel, and tour confirmations saved offline
- [ ] Wallet, cards, and a little Danish krone cash only if preferred; cards are widely used
- [ ] Travel insurance details and emergency contacts
- [ ] Prescription medication in carry-on, with a few extra days’ supply
- [ ] Toiletries, lip balm, moisturizer, and hand sanitizer
- [ ] Charging cables, wall charger, and power bank
- [ ] Type C/F European plug adapter; Greenland uses 230 V / 50 Hz outlets
- [ ] Headphones and an eye mask if you are light-sensitive (days are very long)

## Keep in Carry-on

- [ ] Passport, wallet, medication, charger/power bank, and one warm layer
- [ ] Rain shell and a change of underwear/socks
- [ ] Any camera gear and boat/hiking essentials needed on the first day
- [ ] Snacks and empty water bottle for the flight

## Do Not Overpack

- Heavy parka, snow boots, or technical Arctic gear
- Formal clothes
- A separate outfit for every day — laundry/re-wearing layers is the sane move
- Drones, unless airline rules, local restrictions, and tour operators have been checked first

## Before Leaving

- [ ] Recheck Nuuk weather forecast 2–3 days before departure and adjust insulation
- [ ] Confirm luggage limits and baggage rules with United
- [ ] Confirm boat-tour clothing/gear guidance and cancellation policy
- [ ] Tell someone at home the hiking plan if heading out independently
- [ ] Leave the day after the return flight free if possible; Greenland weather can affect schedules
